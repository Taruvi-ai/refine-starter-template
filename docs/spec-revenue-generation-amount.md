# Revenue Generation Amount

Revenue Generation Kaizens require a `Revenue Generated (USD)` amount greater than zero. Employees enter it during submission; Lead/Manager and OM/SOM may update it during review. The saved amount appears in Overview and every change is captured in Activity Logs.

During OM/SOM review, the reviewer must enter the Kaizen-specific `FTE Cost (USD)`. The system calculates and stores `FTE Saved = Revenue Generated / FTE Cost`. The value is shown live to the OM/SOM and is available to every authorized role after the OM/SOM review is saved. `Cost Saved` remains equivalent to the Revenue Generated amount because it is calculated as `FTE Saved x FTE Cost`.
